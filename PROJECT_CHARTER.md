# Project Charter: Health Without Borders — Frontend

## Vision

A world where every person, regardless of where they were born or where they
live, has access to a continuous, secure, and interoperable health record.

## Mission

Health Without Borders builds open-source software that enables humanitarian
health brigades and migration-context clinicians to collect, store, and share
patient records in low-connectivity environments, using NFC-enabled devices and
offline-first mobile applications.

## Community Statement

Health Without Borders is a community-driven project. We welcome contributors
from any background — clinicians, engineers, designers, translators, and
advocates. We believe that the best software for underserved populations is
built *with* those communities, not just *for* them.

All participants are expected to follow our
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). We are committed to maintaining a
welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment.
