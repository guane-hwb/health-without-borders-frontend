# Administration: Users & Organizations

These screens are for **administrators**. They let you manage the people and organizations that use the platform.

---

## 1. Manage users

Open **Manage users** to see the users in your organization, grouped by role (for example, Administrator, Doctor, Nurse).

From a user's detail you can:

- **Create** a new user.
- **Deactivate** a user — the account is suspended but not removed.
- **Reset the password** of a doctor or nurse (a superadmin can also reset an organization administrator's). The app shows a temporary password **once**: give it to the user, who must replace it when they sign in. All of the user's sessions end.
- **Delete** a user — the account is removed.

---

## 2. Manage organizations

Open **Organizations** to see the list of organizations. Selecting one opens its **details** (identifier, status, number of users, number of patients).

From an organization's detail you can:

- **Deactivate** the organization.
- **Delete** the organization.

**Note:** Deleting an organization **protects patient records** — patient data is never removed. Associated user accounts are deactivated together with the organization.

---

## 3. A note on roles

What you can see and do depends on your role. Organization administrators act within their own organization; super administrators can act across organizations. If an option is not visible to you, your role does not include that permission.
